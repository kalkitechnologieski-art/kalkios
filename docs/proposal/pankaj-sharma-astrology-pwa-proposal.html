1 · Executive summary

You will operate a two-sided astrology platform: users receive accurate, real-time Vedic and occult predictions driven by open-source astrological APIs (Swiss Ephemeris / ephemeris, Ashtakavarga, Dasha and Gochara engines); verified consultants sell their expertise through their own profiles, live per-minute chat, voice and scheduled bookings, with payments settled through Razorpay.

Kalki Intelligence delivers the complete scope — architecture, PWA build, consultant marketplace, payments, hosting, security and ongoing growth marketing — for a flat ₹40,000 per month plus 10% of platform revenue. No licence fees, no paid-infrastructure markup, no separate agency billing. Target live launch: 15–20 December 2026.

2 · Scope of work

User product

  • Installable PWA — works as app and as website from one codebase (no separate app/web build, no store-commission overhead).
  • Kundali, Rashi, Nakshatra, Dasha, Gochar, Ashtakavarga, numerology and Vastu modules from open-source astrology APIs.
  • Siddhi AI astrology concierge — Hindi, English and regional-language answers on birth-chart logic, with citations back to your live data.
  • Report downloads (PDF), horoscope schedule, wallet, referral rewards.

Consultant console

  • Self-service expert profiles, credentials and KYC, pricing per minute, availability calendar.
  • Real-time per-minute chat and call sessions with automatic metering, idle guard and fair mid-session cut-off.
  • Bookings, queue, ratings, earnings dashboard and weekly settlement via Razorpay payouts.
  • Admin panel: verification, commission, disputes, content and fraud controls.

3 · Technology & zero-cost infrastructure policy

Core  Next.js 16 · TypeScript · React Compute-heavy  C++ modules (astronomical ephemeris, chart maths, batch report generation) AI  Open-weight LLM inference, free-tier orchestration with automatic provider fallback Data  Postgres / Supabase, Row-Level Security Realtime  WebSockets for chat, presence and booking queues Payments  Razorpay (UPI · cards · wallets) with GST-compliant invoicing Edge  Cloudflare DNS, WAF, caching

The platform is its own hosting and website surface — the same deployment that serves users also serves your marketing site, consultant pages and SEO landing pages, which removes a second build and a second bill. We build primarily on free and open tiers; your only recurring third-party outlay at launch is domain, hosting, Razorpay transaction charges and Cloudflare. Infrastructure spend then scales with your real footfall and downloads — you pay for growth, not for idle capacity.
